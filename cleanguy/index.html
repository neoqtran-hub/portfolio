First bottle? Start here

Just three steps.

01

Measure

Use the crystal-look cup. Standard load: 0.75 fl oz (cap line). Heavy or extra dirty: 1–1.5 fl oz.

02

Wash

Pour into the drum or drawer. Works great in cold water — HE or standard machines, low suds, no residue.

03

Dry

Pull a sheet from the bottom cap slit and tear. 1 sheet for most loads, 2 for extra softness or odor control.

Tough stains? Rub a small amount directly on the spot, wait 5–10 minutes, then wash.

I've never heard of Clean Guy — why should I trust this brand?

Company scale: millions of units sold across ~30,000 stores (multi-channel retail + DTC). Clean Guy is a PeerBasics brand, alongside Gunk Getter, SMART T, ARRAMOR and PeerBasics.

Parent company established: 2018.

Quality assurance: we partner only with vetted, audited chemical manufacturers. Every production run undergoes batch testing, and we use third-party labs for additional verification.

Are the scents too strong — or floral?

Crafted with perfumers for a balanced, long-lasting finish that won't overpower. Men's-forward options: Pine & Cedar, Sea Salt & Driftwood, and Citrus & Sandalwood.

Prefer none? Try Free & Clear — no fragrance, no added dye.

How does the cost compare to pods and other detergents?

Short answer: it's under $0.25 per wash + dry (100 loads + 100 sheets for under $24.99 when you subscribe).

Versus premium pods, Clean Guy's wash+dry cost is typically lower. Versus premium liquids, it's in the same range once you factor in dryer sheets — with the 2-in-1 convenience built in. Warehouse/value liquids can be cheaper per load, but they don't include sheets and often use simpler enzyme systems.

Can I use this as my everyday detergent, or only for heavy-duty washes?

Yes — use it as your go-to. Most of our customers do. Some keep it as their heavy-duty "fixer" for extra-dirty loads, but it's formulated for everyday laundry too.

  • Everyday loads: 0.75 fl oz (cap line) in cold water.
  • Heavier soil: 1–1.5 fl oz, and consider pre-treating for 5–10 minutes.
  • Dryer sheets: 1 sheet for most loads; 2 for extra softness or odor control.
  • Sensitive skin: choose Free & Clear — no fragrance, no added dye.
How does the built-in dryer-sheet roll work — and what if I need two sheets?

Each 2-in-1 bottle covers wash and dry. The 100-count premium PET dryer sheets live in the bottle base — pull through the bottom slit and tear like a paper towel.

Use 1 sheet for most loads; 2 sheets for extra softness or odor control. If a sheet misfeeds: pull straight out (not upward), keep light tension, and tear against the built-in edge.

Will it actually remove sweat odor and tough stains in cold water?

Yes — built for heavy duty, even cold. The 7-in-1 formula pairs high-performance enzymes with odor-fighting tech, color & fabric protection, low suds / no residue, and cold-water activation.

Pre-treat tip: dab a little on the spot for 5–10 minutes before washing.

Is it safe for my fabrics and my washer?

Designed to be gentle on gear and compatible with modern machines. The low-suds, no-residue formula works in HE and standard washers, and it's septic-safe when used as directed.

Not for garments with organic/protein fibers like wool (and most silks). Avoid dryer sheets on children's sleepwear / flame-resistant items and microfiber towels.
