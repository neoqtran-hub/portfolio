Our Story

We built the laundry aisle we actually wanted.

Half the people doing laundry are men. The aisle has never acted like it.

The problem

Forty feet of lilacs.

Fresh Linen. Morning Blossom. Sparkling Waterfall. Nothing on the shelf was built for a guy coming off a jobsite, a gym, a garage or a twelve-hour shift.

Worse, the stuff barely worked on the mess men actually make. Sweat. Grease. Grass. Dried-in funk that survives a wash and comes back an hour into the day.

Nobody was making laundry for half the population. So we did.

Roy Peer · Founder

The founder

Meet Roy Peer

Roy is a consumer-products entrepreneur who had built and sold before. He noticed the gap the same way everyone does — standing in the aisle, finding nothing.

The difference was that he already knew how to close it. He went straight to a US laundry-science lab and stayed on the bench until the formula outcleaned what was already on the shelf.

Then he did the part nobody else bothered with. He briefed perfumers to make it smell like something a grown man would actually choose.

The fix

Built from the stain up.

No private-label formula with a new sticker on it. Three things had to be true.

01

Clean harder

Six enzymes, each targeting a different mess — sweat and body oil, grass and dirt, grease, food, engine, mud.

02

Smell right eight hours later

Scents briefed like cologne. Top note, heart, base, dry-down — then tested on a shirt at the end of a real day.

03

Take up one shelf spot

100 loads of liquid plus 100 dryer sheets on a roll in the base. One bottle instead of two boxes.
