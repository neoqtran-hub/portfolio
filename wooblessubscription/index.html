FAQs

How long does a beginner kit take?

Complete it in a day or a weekend. Whether you whip through your Wooble in a single sitting or stitch at a slower pace, you'll love the end result.

I'm SERIOUSLY not crafty. Can I really do this?

Yes. We designed The Woobles especially for total beginners. Your kit includes a pre-started piece that lets you dive right into repeatable stitches. As you crochet along with our up-close videos, you'll build your muscle memory and your confidence.

What if I mess up?

No problem at all! Our video tutorials will walk you through each step. If you need extra help, email us at hello@thewoobles.com and we'll help you get untangled.

Can lefties do your kits?

Sure can. Every beginner kit comes with both right- and left-handed step-by-step guides, and all tutorial videos have righty and lefty versions.

What is your return policy?

You've got 14 days from when you receive your kits to return them. Shipping costs aren't refundable, and you'll be responsible for return shipping. To start a return, email returns@thewoobles.com with your order number and reason for return.
